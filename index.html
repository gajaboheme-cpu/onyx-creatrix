03 — Approach

We diagnose
before we design.

What does “holistic digital experience” actually mean?

It means we don’t just look at your website. We look at the whole picture.

What are you selling? How are people finding you? What happens when someone wants to work with you? What are you doing manually? What are you paying for? What’s helping—and what’s making your life harder?

Then we figure out what actually needs to change. Behind the scenes, our Compass helps us see what needs a stronger foundation, what can be simplified, what needs to connect, what deserves more visibility, and what’s ready to grow.

You don’t need to know which platform or technical solution you need. That’s part of what we figure out together.

  • Keep
  • Simplify
  • Connect
  • Clarify
  • Grow

04 — Why Onyx

A website isn’t just information.

For a lot of people, it’s their first experience of your business.

Can they understand you? Can they feel you? Do they trust you? Do they know what to do next?

That’s part of the work too. The right solution isn’t always the most advanced one. We choose what fits now and leave room for your digital systems to grow as the business grows.

You don’t need to build the final version on day one.

05 — About

Behind Onyx

I actually enjoy figuring it out.

I’ve spent 12+ years working behind the scenes in digital marketing—building campaigns, landing pages, email, traffic sources, conversion paths, tracking, and the systems that keep everything moving.

A lot of my job was making sure things were actually working: where people came from, what they clicked, what converted, what didn’t, and where something was breaking. I also became the person who could get dropped into a new system and figure it out pretty quickly. I genuinely like doing that.

Then I started running my own holistic practice. I’ve been building it since 2020, and over time it grew into its own mix of services, experiences, education, websites, booking, email, digital tools, and automation.

That’s when I really understood the other side. I’ve paid for software for months because I knew I was “supposed” to use it while barely touching it. I’ve had the website that needed updating and the platform I knew could help—but didn’t have the time or mental energy to learn.

The difference is, I had the digital background to eventually figure it out. A lot of business owners don’t. That’s a big part of why Onyx exists.
